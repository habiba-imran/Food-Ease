## Final generation prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent photographic food centerpiece for an immersive editorial website. Create a single very large, exquisitely photorealistic crispy chicken burger, completely isolated on a genuinely transparent background, square composition. The burger floats in midair at a slight dynamic 12-degree clockwise tilt, photographed from a low front three-quarter angle. A glossy deeply toasted sesame brioche bun, generous craggy golden fried chicken fillet with real crumb and oil highlights, fresh frilled dark green lettuce, two red tomato slices, one melted pale cheddar layer, creamy sauce, lower bun. The top bun hovers just a little above the fillings, keeping a coherent appetizing burger silhouette rather than a widely exploded diagram. Studio advertising photography with dramatic directional warm key light from upper left, cooler soft fill from right, crisp material detail, convincing shadows between ingredients, sharp macro texture, sculptural dimensionality. Include the entire object with generous clear transparent margin on all sides. Large hero object occupies about 78 percent of square canvas. NO cast background shadow, NO plate, NO table, NO packaging, NO text, NO logos, NO UI, NO background color, NO graphic shapes, NO confetti, NO floating unrelated ingredients. Must look like an actual premium food product photographed for an experimental creative-agency campaign, not a cartoon, not low-poly, not a simplified 3D illustration.
